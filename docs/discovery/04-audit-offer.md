# 2026 Season Recurring-Failure Audit

**For:** one property of [Group name] · **From:** SquareDev · **Date:** [date] · **Valid until:** 15 November 2026

## What it is

A look back over your 2026 season using the records you already have: maintenance work orders, the complaint log, surveys, reviews, stay and occupancy data, and your room and equipment list. We link them by room, building, equipment and time, and show you which defects came back after they were marked fixed, where, how often, and what they cost you in guest goodwill. The output is a report and a prioritised list for your winter works, delivered before the works are locked.

No software is installed. No system is connected. Your data is exported once, with guest and employee identities removed, and deleted after the report is accepted.

## What you get

1. **Recurring failures by room, building and equipment.** Every defect that recurred at the same location during the season, ranked by frequency and by guest impact. Shared-plant problems (a chiller, a hot-water loop, a pump station) are shown as one root cause, not forty room tickets.
2. **Fixes that did not hold.** Work orders closed as fixed where the same problem returned at the same location within 7, 30 and 90 days, by category and by month. Not by technician. No per-person analysis of any kind.
3. **Time to resolve by department and by month.** Including the second-half-of-season effect, when seasonal staff start leaving.
4. **Reviews and surveys tied to operational causes.** Which negative review topics correspond to logged defects, and which do not (problems guests write about that never became a ticket).
5. **A prioritised winter-works list.** The recurring failures ordered by (recurrence × guest impact × cost of failure), with the evidence line for each, written to be pasted into your budget request.
6. **A baseline:** repeat complaints per 1,000 occupied room-nights, and the share of closed work orders that held for 30 days. This is the number the 2027 pilot is measured against.
7. **A records assessment:** what your tools capture well, what they do not, and the five changes to logging practice that would make 2027 data far more useful. Delivered even if the audit stops at the reality check.

Delivery: a written report (PDF), the winter-works list as a spreadsheet, and a two-hour working session with the GM, Director of Operations and Chief Engineer.

## How it leads to the 2027 pilot

The audit gives you a baseline and a list of what to fix. The reopening in April–May 2027 is where the fixes are tested. If the audit shows that your records support it, we propose a live pilot for the 2027 season at the same property: the same linking, running continuously, so that a complaint about a problem "fixed yesterday" is visible to the person on shift, and so that by October 2027 you can see the repeat rate against the 2026 baseline. The pilot is a separate decision, priced separately, and only makes sense if the audit shows something your current tools cannot.
